# Security policy

People are being asked to keep seven years of statutory records in this. The
disclosure process exists from day one, before there is anything worth
disclosing.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the
Security tab, or by email to `security@euro.computer`. Do not open a public
issue.

Include: what you found, how to reproduce it, the version or commit, and what an
attacker gets out of it.

## What to expect

| Stage                              | Target                                          |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Acknowledgement                    | 3 working days                                  |
| Initial assessment with a severity | 10 working days                                 |
| Fix or documented mitigation       | 90 days, sooner for anything actively exploited |

We coordinate disclosure: a fix ships, self-hosters get a window to upgrade, then
the advisory is published with credit to the reporter unless anonymity is asked
for.

## In scope

- Anything that lets one entity read or write another entity's data.
- Anything that breaks the append-only guarantee on the journal, or forges the
  hash chain.
- Anything that exposes adapter credentials, API tokens or documents.
- Privilege escalation across roles, including a scoped token exceeding its
  scope.
- Authentication and session handling.

## Out of scope

- Findings against a deliberately misconfigured instance (default credentials
  left in place, object storage made public, `DEBUG` on in production).
- Denial of service by volume against a self-hosted instance.
- Missing hardening headers with no demonstrated impact.
- Reports generated by a scanner with no analysis attached.

## For self-hosters

- Adapter credentials and secrets are encrypted at rest with a key supplied from
  the environment or a KMS. Never commit one, never store one in plaintext in
  the database.
- Object storage holds statutory records. Turn object lock on. The retention
  requirement is legal, not advisory.
- Keep the audit log exportable and off-box. It is the evidence chain.
