Where documents can come from

A mailbox Forward bills to a mailbox Klopt checks every five minutes. Attachments become documents, the mail itself is kept as proof. Connect a mailbox
A folder Point Klopt at a folder, a scanner's output for example. No password needed, the usual choice for self-hosting. Choose a folder
Peppol e-invoices Suppliers who send e-invoices reach you directly, already read, nothing to fetch. Needs your KvK number in Settings. Finish company details
