Nothing to handle

Drop PDFs, photos or e-invoices anywhere on this page, paste a screenshot with ⌘V, or connect a source below so they arrive by themselves. E-invoices (UBL) are read for you; for a PDF, photo or screenshot you fill in what it says.

Where documents can come from

A mailbox Forward bills to a mailbox Klopt checks for you. Attachments become documents, the mail itself is kept as proof. Connect a mailbox
A folder Point Klopt at a folder, a scanner's output for example. No password needed, the usual choice when you run Klopt on your own server. Choose a folder
Peppol e-invoicesNot set up Once Peppol receiving is set up, suppliers who send e-invoices reach you directly, already read. It needs your KvK number first. Finish company details
